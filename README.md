# TeamSpeak Android (TS3)

![TeamSpeak Android illustrated client previews](docs/images/banner.svg)

**TeamSpeak 3, in your pocket.**

An unofficial TS3 client for Android. Join your usual servers, hang out in voice, and keep up with your chats through a familiar channel tree and a clean, dark interface.

[Download](https://github.com/NotMurPh/teamspeak-android-ts3/releases/latest) | [Features](#features) | [Preview](#preview) | [Feedback](https://github.com/NotMurPh/teamspeak-android-ts3/issues)

## Features

- **Talk your way.** Push to talk, voice activation or an open microphone, with optional volume-key and headset-button controls.
- **Stay in the conversation.** Server, channel and private messages, pokes, notification sounds and spoken announcements.
- **Find your people.** Browse channels, see who's talking, and quickly mute your microphone or sound.
- **Keep your servers close.** Bookmarks, folders, multiple identities and automatic reconnect.
- **Tune your audio.** Noise suppression, echo cancellation, automatic gain control and a built-in microphone test.
- **Use your own setup.** Phone, wired-headset and Bluetooth audio options.
- **More than voice.** Channel files, server-management tools and local backup/restore. Management actions follow your server permissions.
- **Always current.** New releases show up in the app, ready to install in a tap or save to your Downloads folder.

## Installation

**Android 8.0+ | 64-bit ARM (`arm64-v8a`)**

1. Download `teamspeak-android-arm64-v8a.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/NotMurPh/teamspeak-android-ts3/releases/latest).
2. Open the APK to install it. If prompted, allow installation from your browser or file manager.
3. Open **TS Client**, tap **+**, and add your server's address. Grant microphone access to talk.
